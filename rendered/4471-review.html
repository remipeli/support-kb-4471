#4471 · three issues, one ticket

owner remipeli open reviewed 2026-10-10 · 0 days ago

The night of 16 to 17 September, the desk merged 412 reports into #4471 and closed it. Subject matching put all of them under one ticket. The reports show three desk issues, and two of them may share one cause.

What the reports show

The export holds 427 rows. 15 are repeats of an earlier row, so 412 unique reports remain. The desk's code files a report under its place and its error code (fingerprint.ts). Grouping the 412 reports that way gives three desk issues:

IssueReportsReportersNote
sched-prod · E-TZ-114349205Shifts show one hour late.
tram-3 · E-TZ-1144426Departure boards show trams one hour late. Same code as above, different product.
rota-6 · E-SYNC-04199Night shift listed twice. A different code and a different bug.

Probable cause

sched-prod and tram-3 share the code E-TZ-114, the one-hour shift and the time window. The data points to one cause, so issue #2 (tram-3) is closed as a duplicate of #1. rota-6 has a different code. Its copies also sit one hour apart, so it may come from the same shift. The reports do not prove either link.

Why one ticket

The desk merges on subject line (decision 0001). Reports with the same subject went together, even when the product or code differed. Reports with other subjects about the same issue would have been split. The rule does not read the body or the code.

Not separate issues

Some reports also name E-UI-31 or E-NET-03. The desk's code treats these as messages from the client app. No ticket was opened for them.

Open questions

  • Nine report IDs in the range R-41200 to R-41620 are not in the export: R-41207, R-41226, R-41307, R-41326, R-41407, R-41426, R-41507, R-41526, R-41607.
  • Whether one cause explains the one-hour shift in sched-prod, tram-3 and rota-6. The reports alone do not show it.
  • Whether change #29 fixed tram-3 and rota-6. Their reports stop before 06:15, so the data cannot show it.

Lessons

  • Merge on the place and the code. The subject line is not enough.
  • A ticket that holds two issues needs a split before it closes.
